Header: BBC Accreditation Pathways

Present these three tiers clearly so visitors can self-select their starting point:

Level 1: Associate Certified Qualification (ACQ)
Best For: Beginners, managers, HR professionals, and social workers looking to integrate core coaching skills into their career.

What You’ll Learn: Essential coaching frameworks (e.g., GROW model), active listening, goal setting, and professional ethics.

Key Outcome: Ability to conduct structured, ethically sound coaching conversations with confidence.

Duration/Format: [e.g., 6 Weeks | Online & Interactive]

Level 2: Professional Certified Qualification (PCQ)
Best For: Aspiring full-time coaches, specialized consultants, and practitioners ready to build an independent practice.

What You’ll Learn: Advanced cognitive and behavioral strategies, mindset transformation, handling resistance, and customized client journey design.

Key Outcome: Mastery over complex client transformations, deep-level interventions, and independent practice readiness.

Duration/Format: [e.g., 12 Weeks | Advanced Modules & Supervised Hours]

Level 3: Master Certified Qualification (MCQ)
Best For: Experienced coaches, supervisors, authors, and founders creating proprietary methodologies or leading organizations.

What You’ll Learn: Methodological design, clinical-style supervision, intuitive presence, and thought leadership.

Key Outcome: Recognition as an industry authority capable of training, supervising, and mentoring other coaches.

Duration/Format: [e.g., Master Track | Portfolio & Supervision Review]

Yes. BBC frameworks are designed to meet international standards of practice and ethics.
